# Lakeshore Rail: weather and disruption

<!-- Fictional demo data: Lakeshore Rail and the Merrow Valley do not exist. -->

The coastal line between Port Avalon and Kestrel Bay is exposed to strong winds. When forecast gusts exceed 70 km/h, Lakeshore Rail runs a reduced timetable on that section with trains limited to 50 km/h, adding about 15 minutes to the journey.

In heavy snow the Highfold branch, which climbs to 600 metres, may close entirely. Replacement buses then run between Merrow and Highfold, taking about 50 minutes instead of the usual 25 by train.

Weather-related changes are published in the Lakeshore app by 18:00 on the evening before. On days with a weather warning, any ticket for the affected route can be used the following day instead, or refunded without the usual administration fee.
