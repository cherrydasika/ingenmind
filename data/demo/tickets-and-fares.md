# Lakeshore Rail: tickets and fares

<!-- Fictional demo data: Lakeshore Rail and the Merrow Valley do not exist. -->

Lakeshore Rail runs trains between Port Avalon, Merrow, Kestrel Bay and Highfold. Tickets are sold at station machines, in the Lakeshore app and on board. Buying on board costs an extra 4 crowns unless the departure station's machines were out of order.

There are three ticket types. An Anytime ticket is valid on any train on the date shown. An Off-Peak ticket is valid on trains departing after 09:30 and before 16:00 on weekdays, and all day at weekends. An Advance ticket is valid only on the train printed on it and is the cheapest option when bought at least 7 days ahead.

A standard Anytime single from Port Avalon to Highfold costs 38 crowns; the Off-Peak single costs 24 crowns. A return ticket costs the same as two singles.

The Valley Rover pass gives unlimited travel on all Lakeshore Rail trains for 3 or 7 consecutive days, priced at 60 and 120 crowns. Children aged 5 to 15 pay half price on every ticket type; children under 5 travel free and do not need a ticket.

Passengers aged 60 or over can buy a Silver Card for 30 crowns a year, which gives one third off Off-Peak and Advance tickets.
