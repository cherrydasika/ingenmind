# Lakeshore Rail: refunds and delay compensation

<!-- Fictional demo data: Lakeshore Rail and the Merrow Valley do not exist. -->

Unused Anytime and Off-Peak tickets can be refunded within 28 days of the travel date. A 5-crown administration fee is deducted. Refunds go back to the original payment card within 10 working days.

Advance tickets are not refundable, but they can be changed to another train before the original departure time for a 10-crown fee plus any difference in fare.

If a Lakeshore Rail train arrives at your destination late, you can claim compensation regardless of ticket type: 25 percent of the single fare for a delay of 15 to 29 minutes, 50 percent for 30 to 59 minutes, and the full single fare for 60 minutes or more. Claims must be made within 28 days of the journey, in the app or at any staffed ticket office, with the ticket or booking reference.

When a train is cancelled, any ticket for that journey is valid on the next Lakeshore Rail service to the same destination, including on trains that Off-Peak and Advance tickets would normally exclude.
