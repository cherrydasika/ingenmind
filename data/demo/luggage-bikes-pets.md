# Lakeshore Rail: luggage, bicycles and pets

<!-- Fictional demo data: Lakeshore Rail and the Merrow Valley do not exist. -->

Each passenger may bring up to two large items of luggage and one small bag free of charge. Large items must fit in the luggage racks at the end of each carriage; items longer than 120 cm, such as surfboards, need a free Oversize reservation made in the app.

Folding bicycles travel free at any time if folded. Full-size bicycles need a bike reservation, which is free but limited to four bikes per train. Bikes are not carried on weekday trains arriving at Port Avalon between 07:00 and 09:30.

Dogs and cats travel free, up to two per passenger. Dogs must be on a lead and cats in a closed carrier. Animals may not occupy seats. Assistance dogs are always welcome and do not count towards the limit.
