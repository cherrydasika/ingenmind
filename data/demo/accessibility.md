# Lakeshore Rail: accessible travel

<!-- Fictional demo data: Lakeshore Rail and the Merrow Valley do not exist. -->

Every Lakeshore Rail station has step-free access to all platforms. Port Avalon, Merrow and Highfold have staff on duty from the first to the last train; Kestrel Bay is staffed from 06:00 to 20:00.

Passenger Assist provides help boarding and leaving trains, including a ramp. Book it in the app or by phone at least 2 hours before travel; at staffed stations staff will also help without a booking whenever they can.

Each train has two wheelchair spaces in carriage B, next to the accessible toilet. Wheelchair users and one companion get one third off any ticket type.
